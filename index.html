About TripGenie

TripGenie is an AI-powered travel assistant designed to help users quickly discover the best domestic tour packages in India. Instead of manually browsing through multiple travel listings, users can simply chat with TripGenie and share their preferences such as destination, budget, travel duration, travellers, and travel style.

Using a recommendation engine, TripGenie analyzes the user's requirements and suggests the most suitable travel packages, making travel planning faster, easier, and more personalized.
